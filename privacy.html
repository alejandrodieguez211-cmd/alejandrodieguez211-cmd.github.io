Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy covers the What If Universe uploader, a private tool used only by the owner of the What If Universe YouTube channel to upload and schedule the channel's own videos through the YouTube Data API.

What data the tool uses

The tool accesses only the What If Universe channel, with the permission its owner granted through Google. It uploads the channel's videos, sets their titles, descriptions and publishing times, and reads the channel's public statistics. It does not access any other channel, and it does not collect data from viewers or other users.

How data is stored and shared

Access credentials are stored as private environment variables, never published. No data is sold or shared with third parties. Data obtained from YouTube is used only to manage the channel.

Google and YouTube

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

For questions, contact the channel through its YouTube page.
